Our Mission

To preserve and share cultural knowledge, strengthen community leadership, encourage civic education, and advance practical skills in agriculture and self-sufficiency.

Our current status: The Culture Society is being organized. It is not yet represented as an incorporated nonprofit or an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) charity. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible.

What We're Building

Cultural Education

History, heritage, research, and public learning.

Civic Knowledge

Understanding community leadership, government, and civic participation.

Agriculture

Gardening, homesteading, food-growing skills, and self-reliance.

Community Development

Volunteer leadership, member collaboration, and local projects.

Founding Recruitment

Initial membership interest is free. We are inviting people who share the mission to learn about the organization and help shape its future. Formal membership and voting procedures will be governed by adopted bylaws.

We are also seeking prospective volunteer directors. Those appointed as the initial founding directors will be recognized in the organization's historical records.

To express interest, contact The Culture Society through its official social-media account. An online application and contact address will be added when they are ready. Do not send sensitive personal information publicly.

Our Long-Term Vision

To establish a permanent national headquarters in Hattiesburg, Mississippi, with gathering space, educational classrooms, leadership offices, and room for agricultural programs — serving a growing community with a long-term goal of 1,000 members or more.

This is a proposed future goal, not a claim that a facility or funding has already been secured.
